HUNTER ASSOCIATION

Hunter Code of Conduct

By joining the Hunter Association, you officially agree to the following highly important and definitely serious rules:

  1. I agree that I am handsome in all battles, regardless of whether I actually contributed to the team.
  2. I promise to enter every dungeon with 100% confidence and approximately 12% actual preparation.
  3. If I get carried by my party, I will respectfully pretend that it was all part of my strategy.
  4. I agree to never blame the healer, tank, lag, keyboard, mouse, Wi-Fi, or my teammates. (Unless absolutely necessary.)
  5. I shall maintain maximum aura at all times, even when my HP is at 1/100.
  6. If a monster is stronger than me, I will confidently say "I've got this" before immediately reconsidering my life choices.
  7. I promise to look cool while entering the dungeon, even if I have no idea what the quest objective is.
  8. I acknowledge that being defeated does not mean I lost. It means the dungeon was temporarily stronger than me.
  9. I agree that my Hunter ID may be used exclusively for important activities such as looking handsome, clearing dungeons, and collecting unnecessary loot.
  10. Finally, I accept that I am legally, spiritually, and magically required to say:
"I agree. I am handsome in all battles. Now let me enter the dungeon."
